# Photo credits

The 1936 to 1943 photos are from the Library of Congress, Farm Security Administration/Office of War Information collection. The rights statement on every record reads: "No known restrictions on publication." See https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/071_fsab.html.

The 1972 to 1974 color photos are from the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency's DOCUMERICA project, held by the National Archives. They are U.S. government works in the public domain. The files came from the Wikimedia Commons copies of the National Archives records (tagged PD-USGov-EPA). Credit the photographers by name as a courtesy.

The page shows new captions written for this site. The original archive titles are kept below for reference. Some use outdated terms from the 1930s and 1940s; do not show them on the page.

Files were cropped to remove film borders and saved as JPEG. Larger masters are available from each record.
